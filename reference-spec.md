## Visual system

The reference is a dark editorial archive with near-black hero and archive sections, warm bone-paper manifesto and dispatch areas, and restrained oxblood emphasis. The hero is a full-height dark image field with large left-anchored Cormorant-style serif typography, italic oxblood emphasis, a thin uppercase top navigation, small metadata at the top and bottom edges, and a circular arrow CTA. The layout is asymmetric and uses generous negative space, hairline rules, issue numbers, and catalog labels.

The navigation contains VEILHOUSE, Archive, Manifesto, Dispatches, Curator, and Issue 03. The header is persistent across the visual system and the page includes a small Made with Manus badge at the lower right in the reference environment.
